Voice speed test

Tries three small voice makers on this device and times them. Nothing here changes Benny's Hub. Run it in Chrome, the browser Ben uses.

This device

How to use this page

  1. Plugged in, on Wi-Fi: press Download & load on each voice card. Note how long each takes.
  2. Press Run the 3 test sentences on each card. Listen for gaps or stutters.
  3. Turn Wi-Fi off, reload this page, and run them again (they should still work).
  4. Unplug, then use Repeat for 10 minutes at the bottom.
  5. Press Copy results and paste them into a message.

The three sentences:

Pocket TTS: custom voice

Pocket TTS can copy the sound of a short voice clip (up to 10 seconds). Four invented sample voices came with this page, or pick a clip of your own. Only use voices you have permission to copy.

Results

"First sound" is the wait between pressing the button and hearing the voice start (it starts once 0.3 seconds of speech is ready). Targets: short about 1 second or less, medium 1.5, long 2. No gaps.
